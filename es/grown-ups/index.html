How this book works

Read it, or press play and just listen.

  1. Every topic has a code. Scan it with your phone camera, and the book talks. You do not need to speak Spanish.
  2. Tap a picture to hear the word. Choose a woman's voice (Marisol) or a man's voice (Leo), at normal speed or slow.
  3. Say the word together with your child. Then color the picture.
  4. Every topic also has a short folk tale. Your child reads and points, fills in the missing words, puts the sentences in order, and then retells the tale out loud.
  5. Each word shows how to say it in English letters, so the printed book has everything you need. The audio is an extra.
  6. No app. No sign-in. It is free with this book.
